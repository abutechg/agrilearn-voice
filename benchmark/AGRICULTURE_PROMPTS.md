# Agricultural recording prompts

Ask speakers to answer naturally in their own words. They do not need to read the prompt exactly. Record 5-15 seconds per answer.

1. How do I prepare my soil before planting maize?
2. When is the best time to plant maize?
3. How often should I water vegetables?
4. What can I use to control weeds on my farm?
5. How do I make compost at home?
6. How can I improve poor soil?
7. What should I do before planting tomatoes?
8. How do I protect seedlings from strong sun?
9. When should I transplant a seedling?
10. How can I identify healthy maize seeds?
11. What causes yellow leaves on maize?
12. How do I prevent soil erosion?
13. How far apart should I plant maize?
14. How do I store harvested maize safely?
15. What is crop rotation and why is it useful?
16. How can I conserve water during the dry season?
17. What should I do when weeds return quickly?
18. How do I prepare a nursery bed?
19. How can I use animal manure safely?
20. What is the best way to mulch vegetables?
21. How do I protect crops from heavy rain?
22. What should I check before buying seed?
23. How can I reduce pests without harming useful insects?
24. When should I harvest tomatoes?
25. How do I keep tools clean after working on the farm?
26. What crops grow well together?
27. How do I improve drainage in a field?
28. What should I do if maize seedlings are not growing?
29. How do I dry grain after harvesting?
30. What information should I give an agricultural extension officer?
